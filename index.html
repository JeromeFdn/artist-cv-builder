Save as PDF or print

Your browser’s print window opens next. Three settings give the cleanest result:

  1. For a PDF, set Destination to Save as PDF.
  2. Leave Margins on Default. This page sets its own.
  3. Turn off Headers and footers. That removes the web address and date from your pages.

Chrome and Edge number the pages at the bottom right, with your surname. Other browsers may leave pages unnumbered.

Your saved work

Your CV saves automatically in this browser, on this device. It is not stored anywhere else, so it won’t follow you to another computer, and clearing your browsing data erases it. Some browsers, including Safari on iPhone, iPad, and Mac, also erase saved work if you don’t come back to this page for a while. A backup file protects you from that.
